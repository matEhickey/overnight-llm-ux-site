Raw model IDs are a poor first-run decision surface. Model Compass turns the
curated WebLLM list into three responsive, low-commitment starting points:
Quick Start, Everyday Chat, and Capability First.

{/* truncate */}

It is intentionally honest about uncertainty: the cards offer relative guidance
while WebLLM remains responsible for real loading progress.
