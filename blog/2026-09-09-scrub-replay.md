A horizontal timeline strip pinned to the bottom of the chat. Each turn is a tick — click one to jump, drag the scrubber, or hit Play to watch the conversation unfold chronologically. A "Load demo conversation" button seeds 8 turns on first load so the feature works without waiting for a model download.
