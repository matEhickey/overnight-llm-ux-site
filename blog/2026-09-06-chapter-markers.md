Long sessions used to be a wall of bubbles. Chapter Markers lets you slice the conversation into named sections — start a chapter, send messages, end the chapter. Inline dividers appear between sections; a side panel lists every chapter with jump-to and rename; and a one-click export dumps the whole structured conversation as a Markdown file.

**→ [Read the docs](/docs/ux/builds/chapter-markers)** for details and the live demo.
