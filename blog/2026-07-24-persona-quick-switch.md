A top-bar toggle between five predefined personas — Concise Tutor, Creative Storyteller, Code Reviewer, Devil's Advocate, Socratic Coach. Each one swaps the system prompt, picks a tone color, and shows an emoji avatar. Editing the prompt directly drops you into a "Custom" persona until you pick another.
