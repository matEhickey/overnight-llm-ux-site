When a conversation gets long, finding the message you actually wanted to revisit becomes a scroll-and-search chore. **Trail Map** adds a thin horizontal strip just above the chat input: one glyph per committed message, click-to-jump, and a live cursor that tracks the bubble currently in view.

User messages become small dots, assistant messages become bars whose width scales with character count, and an amber outline marks the bubble you're reading right now.
