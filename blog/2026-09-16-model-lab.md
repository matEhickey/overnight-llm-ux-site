Pick two models, type one prompt, watch both stream side-by-side, and see exactly which one was faster, denser, and more structured. Model Lab is the missing "try before you commit" surface for WebLLM — instead of committing to one model in the chat dropdown, you can A/B them on your hardware before going back to the conversation.

The split pane collapses to a stacked column on phones; the metric strip compares TTFT, duration, throughput, length, and response shape with `A wins` / `B wins` / `tie` badges.
