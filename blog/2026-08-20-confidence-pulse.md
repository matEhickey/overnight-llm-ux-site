Every chat UI on the planet treats all messages with the same visual weight. Confidence Pulse flips that: the message currently in your reading center *blooms* (subtle scale + soft shadow), neighbors dim to 78% opacity, and a Tide Meter in the header tracks your scroll position. Pause for 1.2 seconds and the Tide Meter's dot starts a slow breath — the chat is *waiting*. On screens ≥1600px wide, a right-side Confidence Panel shows one dot per message, glowing brighter the more time you've spent reading it.

Fresh axis on the project: *scroll position + idle time → visual density*. Latency Lens measures generation; Adaptive Canvas measures viewport; Shape Memory measures content. Confidence Pulse measures *where the reader is looking*.
