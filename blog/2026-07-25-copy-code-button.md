Every fenced code block in an assistant reply now carries a small "Copy" pill in its top-right corner. Click it and the snippet lands in your clipboard — pill flips to "✓ Copied" for 1.5s, then resets. A hidden-textarea fallback handles non-secure iframe contexts.
