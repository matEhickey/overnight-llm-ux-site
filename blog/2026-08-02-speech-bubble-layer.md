Pivoting from five failed Voice Input cycles: same audio/conversational spirit, different surface. Each assistant reply now pops out as a floating bubble on a 2D workspace canvas — drag the header to move, drag the bottom-right corner to resize, 📌 to pin, ▾ to collapse. A left-rail layer panel (Figma-style) lists every bubble with token estimate, model family, tool-call badge, timestamp, and response shape. Positions persist to localStorage per message-id. Click "🪟 Layer" in the header to flip on.
