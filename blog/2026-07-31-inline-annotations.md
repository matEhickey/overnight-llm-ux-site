Inline Annotations lets you select any text fragment inside an assistant message, click a floating 📝 button that appears next to your selection, and write a margin note that persists in your browser's localStorage. Annotated fragments get a yellow underline; click them to toggle the note's tooltip. Edit, delete, and survive a page reload — purely client-side, no model call, works with the default 1B model.

**→ [Read the docs](/docs/ux/builds/inline-annotations)** for details and to try the live demo.
