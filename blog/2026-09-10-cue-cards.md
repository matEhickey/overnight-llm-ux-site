A horizontal strip of one-tap intent modifier chips — Be concise, Use bullet points, ELI5, Add a code example, and more — pinned above the input. Pick a cue, type your prompt, send; the model sees your text plus a short instruction line. Cues are ephemeral (per-turn), prefix-only (no system-prompt mutation), and zero-persistence (no localStorage). Toggles accumulate, auto-reset after send, and feed directly into Reply Receipt's `responseShape` classifier.

**→ [Read the docs](/docs/ux/builds/cue-cards)** for details and to try the live demo.
