Every conversation has been disposable until now: refresh and your session vanishes. The Conversation Library captures every chat in localStorage, auto-extracts topic tags from the first user message, and offers full-text search with hit highlighting, date/topic/model filters, jump-to-message navigation, and JSON or Markdown export. Comes pre-seeded with six demo sessions covering polyrhythms, TypeScript, SQL, React refactors, modal logic, and French translation, so the panel is interesting the moment it opens.
