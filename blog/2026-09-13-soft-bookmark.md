A 🔖 ribbon on every assistant reply turns good answers into a personal, localStorage-backed archive that survives resets, reloads, and model swaps. Click the ribbon to save the snippet; click the new `🔖 Bookmarks (n)` chip in the header to open the slide-out panel, filter by model, and `↗ Jump` straight back to the source bubble. Panel becomes a bottom sheet on phones; ribbon collapses to icon-only at the same breakpoint. Ships three curated demo bookmarks so the panel is populated the moment the iframe loads.

**→ [Read the docs](/docs/ux/builds/soft-bookmark)** for details and to try the live demo.
