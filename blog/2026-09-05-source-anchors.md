Each assistant reply now scans itself for sources — bare URLs, `[N]` footnotes, "according to X" attributions, year-with-context anchors — and surfaces them as a horizontal chip strip. Click a chip and the bubble flashes amber; on phones the chips collapse into a `📌 N sources` pill that opens a bottom sheet.
