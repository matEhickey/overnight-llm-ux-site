A live `✨ Refine` chip strip appears below the chat input as you type. Up to three heuristic hints fire automatically — `TL;DR request`, `Specify length`, `Specify language`, `Concrete subject`, `Capitalise first word`, `Quantify if you can`, `Specify output format`, `Add a constraint`. Click a chip to splice its template at the textarea cursor and keep typing. Pure local heuristic — no model call, no API hit, zero latency, no persistence.

**→ [Read the docs](/docs/ux/builds/prompt-refiner)** for details and to try the live demo.
